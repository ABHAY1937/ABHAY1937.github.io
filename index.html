01 / ABOUT

My start in AI wasn't about training models — it was frustration watching good research fail in the real world because it was too slow, too expensive, or too fragile to trust. At Safetywhat, an early-stage startup, I learned how systems actually behave under pressure: what breaks at 2am, what a client actually needs versus what a paper promises.

That taught me where real innovation happens — at the constraints. How do you run 3D posture estimation on a device with no GPU? How do you fit a large language model into a serverless deployment that costs pennies instead of dollars an hour? More recently: how do you let an AI agent safely take action inside a company's actual ERP, instead of just talking about it?

Vision · Edge

CPU-first computer vision and knowledge distillation, built for hardware that has real limits.

Language · Agents

RAG pipelines and multi-agent systems that stay grounded, fine-tuned rather than prompted-and-hoped.

Agentic · RPA

Agentic RPA on open-source computer-use tooling (Cua), MCP tool servers and LLM gateways that connect agents to ERP and business systems — without per-bot licensing.

Discovery · ROI

Business-analyst groundwork first: mapping processes, finding automation gaps, and sizing scope and ROI before anything gets built.

Evals · LLMOps

AI evals and LangSmith tracing to measure agent accuracy and catch hallucinated tool calls before they reach production.

Client work stays private, but I build in the open too — multi-agent tooling, vision-language research and real-time systems are on my GitHub. Either way, the goal is the same: end-to-end systems that turn hard constraints into an advantage.
