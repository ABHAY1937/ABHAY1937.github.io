Who I Am.

My journey in AI didn't start with just training models; it started with a frustration: seeing brilliant research fail in the real world because it was too slow, too expensive, or too fragile. At Safetywhat, I learned firsthand about early-stage startups—the ups and downs, how systems truly work in the wild, how they fail under pressure, and how every day presents a new lesson. I don't just build AI; I engineer it to survive and thrive in production.

I realized early on that true innovation happens at the constraints. How do you run complex Computer Vision—like fluid action recognition or 3D posture mapping—on edge devices with zero GPUs? How do you take a massive LLM and squeeze it into a serverless deployment that costs pennies a day instead of dollars an hour?

This is where I operate. I specialize in:

  • Vision at the Edge: Pushing the limits of CPU optimization and knowledge distillation, ensuring high-FPS intelligence where hardware is restricted.
  • Scalable Language: Architecting autonomous, multi-agent RAG systems and fine-tuning quantized models to deliver human-like, scalable NLP interactions.
  • Rapid Prototyping & Client Strategy: Leading rapid POC development to validate complex AI architectures fast, while translating deep technical capabilities into high-impact client presentations and strategic meetings.

My codebase is strictly private because the architectures I design are highly proprietary and explicitly built to have a massive societal and business impact. I don't just string models together; I build robust, end-to-end software ecosystems that turn complex constraints into competitive advantages.
